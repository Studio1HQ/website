# Archived public assets

Files moved out of `public/` because nothing on the site references them. They
are kept here, not deleted, and are not served. To bring one back, move it into
the right folder under `public/assets/` and reference it from there.

- Client and partner logos no longer shown: amazon, fabric, flyte, gibson,
  lambdatest, meilisearch, unacademy, uploadcare.
- Testimonial headshots no longer shown: Alexandr, Dani, Eddie, Jim, Rosie,
  philip-vollet.
- Older team images: arindam-nobg / arindam_nobg (old cut-outs),
  amitesh-anand, amitesh-profile-cutout, and `dither/` (the earlier dot-only
  portraits).
- Source photos for the team portraits: arindam.png, souradeep.png,
  kathank.jpg, Bhavya Bhardwaj.jpeg. The site uses the processed copies in `public/assets/team/`.
- image-1.webp: a stray upload from the site root.

`public/assets/` is organised as:

- `logos/`: client and partner logos and wordmarks (`logos/white/` for the
  white versions shown on dark backgrounds).
- `people/`: client headshots used in testimonials and case studies.
- `team/`: Studio1 team portraits and photos.
- `site/`: the Studio1 logo and site graphics.
