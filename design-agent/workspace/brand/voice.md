# Studio1 voice

Studio1 is a developer marketing and DevRel agency. We write like engineers explaining something to
other engineers: plain, specific and short. Sources: `brand/README.md` (Writing voice) and
`brand/AI-SYSTEM-PROMPT.md` in the website repo, plus the founders' rules marked below.

## Who we write for

- DevTools companies at every stage. Never "early-stage" only, and never only AI or open source.
- Studio1 helps them get found and adopted by developers through technical content, docs, video,
  launches and community programs.

## Rules

- Numbers and names over adjectives. "400+ deliverables, 29 framework integrations" beats "massive
  impact". Use only the numbers and names the person gave you; never invent a metric.
- "We" is Studio1. "You" is the reader.
- Short sentences, one idea each.
- No hype words: revolutionary, game-changing, unlock, supercharge, leverage, cutting-edge, seamless.
- No emoji. No exclamation marks. No em dashes or en dashes: use a comma, a full stop or the word "to". This is a house rule from the founders, and the lint enforces it.
- Headlines and buttons are Title Case ("Ready to Scale Developer Adoption?"). Body copy and captions
  are sentence case. Uppercase is only for small eyebrow labels.
- Stats are written 35+, 3M+. Sublines split items with a middle dot: Content · Docs · Video ·
  DevRel programs · Launches.

## Spelling

- Studio1: one word, capital S, the digit 1. Never "Studio 1", "studio1" or "STUDIO1". Handle
  @Studio1HQ, site studio1hq.com.
- DevRel, DX, GitHub.
- devtool and devtools are lowercase in sentences. "DevTools" is capitalised only as a headline
  accent, as in "Developer Marketing & DevRel Agency for DevTools".
- open source (noun), open-source (adjective).

## Other agencies (founders' rule)

Never name, link to or show another agency, including its case studies. If a piece needs an example,
use Studio1's own work.

## Client work that is not public (founders' rule)

Nebius and Bright Data must never appear in anything made for outside eyes: not in copy, not on a
graphic, not in a screenshot, a chart label or a file name. Treat their work as client work that is
not public. If a request names either, say so and ask the person for a different example.

## Real people, real work

Never generate a person to stand in for the team, a speaker or a client. Never invent client logos,
dashboards or numbers.
