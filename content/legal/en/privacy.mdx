## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how **STUDI01HQ PRIVATE LIMITED**, a company incorporated in India and trading as **Studio1** ("Studio1", "we", "us" or "our"), collects, uses, shares and protects personal information through studio1hq.com (the "Website").

Studio1 is the Data Fiduciary for this information under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the "DPDP Act"). Where the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation applies, Studio1 is the data controller.

Personal information shared as part of a client engagement is also governed by the agreement with that client.

## 2. Information we collect

**Enquiries and calls.** Your name, email address, company and the contents of your message. Calls are scheduled through Cal.com, which collects the details you enter there.

**Job applications.** Your name, email address, location, LinkedIn and portfolio links, links to your work, résumé (PDF), availability and answers to the application questions.

**DevRel Cohort applications.** Your name, email address, location, current role, profile and work links, answers to the application questions, time commitment and how you heard about the program.

**Hackathon registrations.** Your name, email address, country, role, GitHub and website links, experience level, team details, chosen track, project idea and how you heard about the event.

**Consent records.** Each form records your consent to store your details, whether you opted in to future emails and, where offered, whether you agreed to your application being shared with partner companies.

**Website usage.** We use Raah, a web analytics tool built and operated by Studio1, to understand how the Website is used. It records pages viewed, the referring website, browser and device type, and approximate location, and does not use cookies. Our hosting and security providers also keep standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and request time, to keep the Website secure and available.

We do not request sensitive information such as health, financial or government identification details. Please do not include such information in forms or emails.

## 3. How we use your information

We use personal information to:

- respond to enquiries, schedule calls and share proposals;
- deliver and manage services for our clients;
- review job, cohort and hackathon applications, and contact you about them;
- run the programs and events you take part in;
- send updates about future roles, cohorts and events, **only where you have opted in**;
- share your application with partner companies, **only where you have agreed** (see section 5);
- understand how the Website is used and improve it; and
- keep the Website and our systems secure, and meet our legal obligations.

## 4. Basis for processing

- **Consent.** Our forms ask for your consent before your details are stored. Email updates and partner sharing are optional and recorded separately. You may withdraw your consent at any time by writing to us.
- **Contract.** To prepare for and perform services requested by you or your organisation.
- **Legitimate uses.** To keep the Website secure and to measure its use in aggregate.
- **Legal obligations.** Where we are required by law to retain or disclose information.

## 5. Sharing of information

Studio1 does not sell personal information. We share it only with:

- **Service providers** that support the Website and our operations, including Vercel (hosting), Cloudflare (content delivery and security), Supabase (form data and file storage), Cal.com (call scheduling) and our email provider. They process information only to provide their services to us.
- **Partner companies, with your consent.** If you agree on the DevRel Cohort form, we may share your application with companies hiring for developer relations roles. If you agree on a hackathon form, we may share your details with that hackathon's partners.
- **Government or regulatory authorities**, where required by law.

Some service providers may process information outside India, subject to their contractual and security obligations.

## 6. Data retention

We retain personal information only for as long as it is needed for the purpose for which it was collected, or as required by law. It is then deleted or anonymised. You may ask us to delete your information or unsubscribe from our emails at any time.

## 7. Data security

We use reasonable security measures to protect personal information. Access to form submissions is restricted to authorised team members, and résumés are stored in private storage that is not publicly accessible.

## 8. Cookies and similar technologies

The Website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

- **Language preference.** A cookie named `studio1-locale` remembers the language you select.
- **Analytics.** Raah uses your browser's local and session storage instead of cookies.
- **Third-party content.** The About page includes an embedded YouTube video, and YouTube may set its own cookies when it loads. Our booking link opens Cal.com, which is governed by its own privacy policy.

You can clear cookies and site data at any time through your browser settings.

## 9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

- access a summary of the personal information we hold about you;
- request correction, completion or updating of your information;
- request erasure of your information;
- withdraw your consent;
- nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity, under the DPDP Act;
- object to or restrict certain processing, or request a copy of your data in a portable format, where the GDPR applies; and
- raise a grievance with us and, if it is not resolved, approach the Data Protection Board of India or, in the EU or UK, your local data protection authority.

To exercise these rights, please contact our Grievance Officer (section 12). We aim to respond within 30 days.

## 10. Children

The Website and our programs are intended for people aged 18 and over. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child without verifiable parental consent, we will delete it.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated.
