Studio1 supported **ScrapeGraphAI** with a developer growth campaign across content, demos, community, and events: built to increase awareness and make the product easier for developers to understand.

The campaign combined **social distribution**, **hands-on demo projects**, **event activation**, and **developer experience feedback**, reaching **265K+ developers** across social and community channels.

## About ScrapeGraphAI

[**ScrapeGraphAI**](https://dub.sh/scrapegraph) is a developer-first AI scraping and data extraction platform that lets teams build intelligent agents for web research, structured data collection, and automation. It provides:

* AI-powered web scraping
* Structured data extraction
* Browser automation
* API-based access to scraped data

Because the product sits at the intersection of AI agents and data infrastructure, developers need to see it solving real problems in production, not read a feature list, before they trust it inside their own stack.

## The challenge: proving real-world use over feature marketing

ScrapeGraphAI needed a campaign that could:

* Increase **awareness and reach** across developer communities beyond its existing audience
* Show **real, practical use-cases** in place of abstract feature marketing
* Drive **hands-on adoption** of its SDK and APIs
* Improve **product clarity, docs, and onboarding** for new users
* Strengthen positioning with its ICP: **AI engineers, founders, and agent builders**

The brief wasn't more content. It was a campaign that connected product education with developer attention.

## Our role

Studio1 supported ScrapeGraphAI across:

* **Social distribution** for developer audiences
* **Demo engineering** for practical developer use-cases
* **Visual storytelling**: illustrations and explainers that made agent architecture legible at a glance
* **Event presence** through in-person developer conversations
* **Developer experience feedback** based on real usage

Every piece was built to show useful product context, not just collect impressions.
