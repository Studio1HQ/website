Studio1 does DevRel and developer marketing for devtool, AI, and open-source teams. Every week we ship tutorials, docs, videos, social posts, and launches for several clients at once, with engineers, writers, designers, and video creators working on each one.

We are hiring a Project Coordinator to keep all of that moving. You make sure every client knows what's happening, every person on the team knows what to do next, and nothing slips between the cracks.

## What you will do

- Run the day-to-day for client accounts: timelines, the weekly plan, and what each person on the team is working on.
- Turn client requests into clear tasks and briefs for our engineers, writers, designers, and video creators, and follow up until they ship.
- Track every piece of work from idea to published, and flag delays early, not on the deadline.
- Send weekly updates to clients and put together monthly reports: what shipped, the results, and what's next.
- Take notes on client calls and turn them into action items.
- Help with distribution: post and share client and Studio1 content in the right places, and track how each piece did.
- Help with events, hackathons, and partner activities: logistics, schedules, and follow-ups.
- Keep our internal tools and docs tidy so anyone can see the status of any project in a minute.

## What we are looking for

- High agency: you don't wait to be told what to do. You spot what needs doing, figure it out, ship it, and own the result.
- 0-2 years coordinating projects, events, clients, or a team: at a startup, an agency, a college club, or a community. Show us something you kept on track.
- Very organised. You keep track of many small things across many people without dropping any.
- Clear, polite writing in English. A client or a teammate reads your message once and knows what to do.
- Comfortable with tools such as Notion, Google Sheets, Slack, and a task tracker, and with AI tools to save time.
- Curious about tech. You don't need to code, but you can read a product's landing page or docs and understand what it does.
- A big plus: agency experience, especially at a marketing, content, or developer marketing agency, handling several clients at once.
- A plus: experience in developer communities, tech events, or content.

## Why join

You will see how a developer marketing agency runs from the inside, across many clients and many kinds of work, and you will grow with the company as it scales.

## Role details

This is a full-time remote role for India-based candidates. You will work closely with the founders and the entire team.
