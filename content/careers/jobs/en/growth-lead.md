Content without distribution is just a draft sitting in a folder, so we are hiring someone to own how our work, and our clients' work, gets found and shared.

This is a growth role for technical products. Your job is to get the right developers and buyers to see our work and turn that attention into leads. You will work closely with our Content Lead: they own what we make, you own where it goes and what it brings in.

## Why this role exists

One post from X that sums up how we think about it: for early-stage tech companies, community, founder-led content, and social media are the channels that work.

## What you will do

- Distribute our work where developers already are: Reddit, Hacker News, X, LinkedIn, Dev.to, daily.dev, and Discord and Slack communities, within each community's rules.
- Find the angles and hooks that make technical work spread: launches, open-source projects, tutorials, benchmarks, and opinions.
- Run Studio1's launches and bring the same playbook to client launches and campaigns.
- Build our distribution system from scratch for how developer communities work now: what goes where, when, and in which format, on a weekly rhythm.
- Turn attention into pipeline: inbound DMs, calls booked, and client leads, with the founders on follow-up.
- Notice platform and format shifts early, such as what X rewards now or what a subreddit will and won't accept, and adjust every week.
- Use AI tools such as Claude Code, Grok, ChatGPT, and automation to research communities, track conversations, and ship faster.
- Review what worked every week and do more of it. We measure inbound DMs, calls booked, client leads, and replies and follows from the right people, not views alone.
- Hire and guide interns as needed to scale distribution.

## What we are looking for

- High agency: you don't wait to be told what to do. You spot what needs doing, figure it out, ship it, and own the result.
- 1+ year in growth or marketing for a technical product: a devtool, AI, SaaS, API, or open-source project. Experience only with general consumer brands is not a fit.
- Proof in public: launches, posts, or campaigns you ran that spread, and you can explain why they worked.
- You think distribution-first, and you know how developer communities react to brands and what not to post: no spam, no vote manipulation.
- You are comfortable with technical topics. You can read docs and READMEs and talk to engineers.
- You use AI tools every day and check what they produce.
- Launch experience on Product Hunt or Hacker News, or with SEO, is a plus, and so is having guided interns or freelancers.

## Role details

This is a full-time remote role for India-based candidates. You will work closely with the founders, our Content Lead, and our engineers, writers, and designers.

## How to apply

Apply with the form on this page. Have these ready:

1. Links to launches, campaigns, or channels you have run or grown. Numbers help.
2. How you would get one recent Studio1 post or case study in front of the right developers in its first week.
3. Three angles you would use to launch a developer tool of your choice.

We usually respond within 1 week if there is a fit.

**We care more about judgment and proof of work than about a perfect CV.**
