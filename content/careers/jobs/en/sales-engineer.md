Studio1 is hiring a Sales Engineer: technical enough to talk architecture with a CTO, and commercial enough to turn that conversation into work they want to buy. You will work directly with the founders on every deal.

## Why this role exists

Our buyers are technical. A founder building an inference platform or an agent framework wants to talk to someone who understands why developers aren't adopting it yet: the quickstart takes forty minutes, the docs skip the one integration everyone needs, nobody has seen it working in a real app.

When we can name the blocker, the plan follows: tutorials, sample apps, docs, videos, a launch, community work. This role makes sure every first call ends with that clarity. Our [case studies](/case-studies) show the kind of plans we deliver.

## What you will do

- Run discovery calls with founders, CTOs and heads of growth or DevRel at devtool and AI startups.
- Try the product before the call: sign up, follow the quickstart, read the docs and the repo, and come in knowing where developers get stuck.
- Turn what you find into a proposal: what we would build and publish, in what order, and how we would measure adoption. Work out scope and pricing with the founders.
- Bring the right proof to each conversation: a demo, a tutorial, a case study, or the person on our team who built something similar.
- Find and qualify early-stage infrastructure and AI companies through LinkedIn, investor portfolios, accelerator batches, GitHub and developer communities such as Reddit and Discord.
- Build relationships with VCs, accelerators and ecosystem partners who can introduce us to the companies they back.
- Hand new clients over to the team doing the work with the technical context, so week one isn't spent asking the same questions again.
- Keep every deal up to date in our CRM, and keep the founders posted each week.

## What we are looking for

- High agency: you don't wait to be told what to do. You spot what needs doing, figure it out, ship it, and own the result.
- 2 to 4 years in sales engineering, solutions engineering or consulting, or DevRel with a commercial side.
- Hands-on with modern infrastructure and AI: a cloud provider, containers and Kubernetes, CI/CD, infrastructure as code such as Terraform, and LLM APIs, RAG and agents. You don't need to be an expert in all of it, but you can get a demo running on your own.
- You can explain a technical product's value to a founder in plain words, and hold your own with their engineers.
- Clear writing: proposals and follow-ups that a busy founder reads to the end.
- You use AI tools every day, and you check what they produce.
- A plus: go-to-market experience at a SaaS or devtool company, an active presence in developer communities (GitHub, Discord, Reddit, Hacker News), or a network in the startup and investor scene.

## How we'll measure it

- Discovery calls that end with a clear next step.
- Proposals sent, and how many turn into signed work.
- Introductions from investors and partners.
- New clients who start quickly because the plan and the context were clear from the first week.

## Role details

This is a full-time remote role for India-based candidates. You'll work with the founders, our Sales Development Representative (we're hiring for that role too), and the engineers, writers and designers who deliver the work you scope.

## How to apply

Apply with the form on this page. Have these ready:

1. A devtool or AI product with a public quickstart that you tried: where a new developer gets stuck, and the three things you would make to fix it.
2. A deal, integration or customer problem you worked through end to end, and what you did.
3. A link to something technical you built, wrote or demoed: a repo, a talk, a post or a video.

We usually respond within 1 week if there is a fit.

**We care more about judgment and proof of work than about a perfect CV.**
