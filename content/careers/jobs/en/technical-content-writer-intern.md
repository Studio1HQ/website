Studio1 works with devtool, AI, and SaaS teams on technical content, DevRel, product launches, docs, and developer growth. Alongside client work, we also work on products like Raah and AssetSnip.

We are looking for a Technical Content Writer Intern who can track what is trending in AI and developer tooling and turn it into clear, well-researched, SEO-optimized blog content. This is not a project-building or demo-coding role. It is a research-and-writing role for someone with a technical background who can read papers, docs, release notes, and community discussions on LLMs and adjacent topics, then explain them accurately for a developer audience while writing in a way that ranks and reads well.

## What you will do

You will turn trending technical topics into SEO-friendly blog posts that hold up to a technical reader's scrutiny.

- Track trending topics in LLMs, AI agents, developer tools, and the broader AI/devtool ecosystem to find timely blog angles.
- Research releases, papers, docs, changelogs, and community discussions to understand a topic accurately before writing about it.
- Write SEO-optimized blog posts, explainers, comparison posts, and trend roundups for Studio1 and product blogs.
- Structure posts around keyword intent, headings, and readability best practices without sacrificing technical accuracy.
- Work with the Studio1 team to identify content gaps, keyword opportunities, and topics with strong search or community interest.
- Fact-check technical claims, cite sources, and keep explanations accurate even when simplifying for a broader audience.
- Iterate on drafts based on editor and SEO feedback to improve clarity, structure, and search performance.

## What we are looking for

You should have a technical or software background and be comfortable researching and writing about AI/LLM topics, even if you are not building projects day to day.

- A technical, engineering, or software background (coursework, degree, projects, or work experience) that lets you understand LLM, AI, and developer topics without hand-holding.
- Interest in following AI/LLM news, model releases, and developer tooling trends closely.
- Some experience or strong interest in SEO writing, keyword research, or content that is written to perform in search.
- Proof of writing ability from blogs, newsletters, coursework, technical write-ups, or personal projects; formal publishing experience is a plus but not required.
- Ability to research unfamiliar or fast-moving topics quickly and explain them clearly without oversimplifying or getting facts wrong.
- Comfortable using AI tools to assist research and drafting, while keeping the final writing accurate, original, and in your own voice.

## Why join

You will build a public portfolio of technical, SEO-driven writing on some of the fastest-moving topics in AI, while working closely with the Studio1 team on real blog and content strategy for Studio1, Raah, and AssetSnip.

## Internship details

This is a 3-month remote internship for India-based candidates, with extension possible based on performance and work quality. Best fit: someone with a technical background who enjoys research and writing more than day-to-day project building.

## How to apply

Use the Apply button and keep the subject line as: `Application: Technical Content Writer Intern`.

Include your resume, past writing samples if you have them, and one short blog draft or outline on a trending LLM or AI topic of your choice, optimized for search.

We usually respond within 1 week if there is a fit.

**We ask for this because writing accuracy and SEO instinct matter more than formal publishing experience for this role.**
