Studio1 does DevRel and developer marketing for devtool, AI, and open-source teams. We have worked with 35+ teams and driven 3M+ content views. We are hiring someone to create what we publish, in writing and on video: for Studio1's channels, the founders' profiles, and our clients, every week, in a voice that sounds like real people with real opinions.

This is a hands-on role for someone who has already done developer marketing, not someone who wants to learn it here. You know what a good X article looks like, why one Reddit post gets 500 upvotes and another gets removed, and how a developer goes from reading a post to running `npm install`. You create the content yourself, posts and short videos, and run the system that keeps it coming.

## Why this role exists

One post from X that sums up how we think about it: the answer to AI slop is not posting less. It's posting better, more often.

## What you will do

- Write X articles, threads, and posts that developers save and share, for Studio1 and the founders' profiles. Long-form X articles are a big part of this.
- Write Reddit posts and comments that belong in the subreddit: useful first, honest about who we are, and within each community's rules.
- Make founder-led content work: pull the founders' experiences and opinions out of them and turn them into posts, without the founders writing everything.
- Build an audience from the ground up: pick the topics we want to be known for, post on them every week, and grow followers who are the developers and devtool founders we want to reach.
- Write content that gets developers to try a product: launch posts, "how I built this" stories, quickstarts turned into posts, comparison posts, and short demos.
- Pitch developers the way developers like to be pitched: show the code, the result, and the tradeoffs, with no marketing speak.
- Make short videos: script, record, and edit Shorts, reels, and clips on new AI and developer tools, and quick product demos.
- Create content for our clients' socials and founders, in each client's voice.
- Turn one strong piece into many: an X article becomes a thread, a LinkedIn post, a Reddit post, a short clip, and a newsletter section.
- Build our content system: ideas, briefs, drafts, edits, publishing, and repurposing on a weekly rhythm, so output doesn't depend on a good week.
- Use AI tools such as Claude Code, Grok, ChatGPT, and automation to research, draft, and repurpose faster. Cut anything that reads generic.
- Give our copywriter and interns clear briefs and feedback.
- Review what worked every week and do more of it.

## What we are looking for

- High agency: you don't wait to be told what to do. You spot what needs doing, figure it out, ship it, and own the result.
- 1+ year creating content for a technical product: a devtool, AI, SaaS, API, or open-source project. Experience only with general consumer brands is not a fit.
- Proof in public. X articles, threads, or posts that spread, on your own profile or one you wrote for, and you can explain why they worked.
- Reddit experience: posts that did well in developer subreddits without being removed, and a feel for what each community will and won't accept.
- You have grown an account or a community from a small start, and can show the before and after.
- You have done devtool marketing: a launch, a product's developer content, or getting developers from a post to signing up or starring a repo.
- You have taste. You can rewrite a flat hook and say why in one sentence.
- You are comfortable with technical topics. You can read docs and READMEs, try a product yourself, and talk to engineers.
- You can make short videos yourself: script, record, and edit. They don't need to be polished, they need to be clear and worth watching.
- You use AI tools every day and check what they produce.
- A plus: ghostwriting for founders, writing for client accounts, a newsletter, or Hacker News or Product Hunt launches.

## Role details

This is a full-time remote role for India-based candidates. You will work closely with the founders and the entire team.

## How to apply

Apply with the form on this page. Have these ready:

1. Three pieces you made that spread, with links and numbers, and why each one worked.
2. A rewrite of one recent Studio1 post from X or LinkedIn, with what you changed and why.
3. The X post and the Reddit post you would use to get developers to try a developer tool of your choice.
4. A short video you made.

We usually respond within 1 week if there is a fit.
