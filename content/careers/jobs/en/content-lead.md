We are hiring someone to own what we publish: Studio1's channels and the founders' personal profiles, every week, in a voice that sounds like real people with real opinions.

This is a content role for technical audiences. You create and ship content yourself, and you run the system that keeps it coming. You will work closely with our Growth Lead: you own what we make, they own where it goes and what it brings in.

## Why this role exists

One post from X that sums up how we think about it: the answer to AI slop is not posting less. It's posting better, more often.

## What you will do

- Keep every profile active and consistent: Studio1's channels (mainly X and LinkedIn, plus our blog, newsletter, and short video) and the founders' personal profiles.
- Make founder-led content work: pull the founders' experiences and opinions out of them and turn them into posts, without the founders writing everything.
- Create and ship content yourself, and coordinate with the right person on the team when a piece needs design, video, or engineering input.
- Turn one strong piece into many: a thread, a post, a clip, a community post, and a newsletter section.
- Build our content system from scratch: ideas, briefs, drafts, edits, publishing, and repurposing on a weekly rhythm, so output doesn't depend on a good week.
- Use AI tools such as Claude Code, Grok, ChatGPT, and automation to research, draft, and repurpose faster. Cut anything that reads generic.
- Lead a lean team: coordinate with our engineers, writers, designers, and video team, and hire and guide interns as needed to scale our content.
- Review what worked every week with the Growth Lead and do more of it.

## What we are looking for

- High agency: you don't wait to be told what to do. You spot what needs doing, figure it out, ship it, and own the result.
- 1+ year creating content for a technical product: a devtool, AI, SaaS, API, or open-source project. Experience only with general consumer brands is not a fit.
- Proof in public. Your own X or LinkedIn, or a channel you grew, shows posts that spread, and you can explain why they worked.
- You have taste. You can rewrite a flat hook and say why in one sentence.
- You are comfortable with technical topics. You can read docs and READMEs and talk to engineers.
- You use AI tools every day and check what they produce.
- Short-form video, newsletter, or writing for founders is a plus, and so is having guided interns or freelancers.

## Role details

This is a full-time remote role for India-based candidates. You will work closely with the founders, our Growth Lead, and our engineers, writers, and designers.

## How to apply

Apply with the form on this page. Have these ready:

1. Links to content or channels you have owned or grown. Numbers help.
2. A rewrite of one recent Studio1 post from X or LinkedIn, with what you changed and why.
3. Three first-draft posts for the founder of a developer tool of your choice.

We usually respond within 1 week if there is a fit.

**We care more about judgment and proof of work than about a perfect CV.**
