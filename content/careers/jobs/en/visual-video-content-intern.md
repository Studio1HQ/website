Studio1 needs a Content Creator Intern who can turn rough ideas into clean memes, visuals, scripts, reels, and short videos. You will work with the Studio1 team to grow Studio1 socials and support product content for Raah and AssetSnip.

## What you will do

You will own the visual and production side of social growth, from scripts and edits to scheduled assets.

- Create memes, scripts, reels, carousels, visual explainers, cover graphics, short videos, and campaign graphics.
- Use Canva, Figma, AI creative tools such as HeyGen, Higgsfield, or similar video tools as part of the workflow.
- Package, schedule, and repurpose blogs, docs, product updates, and launch ideas into polished social assets.

## What we are looking for

You do not need agency experience. You should have taste, speed, and proof that you like making visual content.

- You have created memes, reels, edits, carousels, cover graphics, motion posts, or design samples.
- A tech or software background helps a lot, but curiosity and willingness to learn matter too.
- You are comfortable using AI video or design tools, especially HeyGen-style tools.
- You can take a rough content idea and make it look clear, sharp, and shareable.

## Why join

You will work with the Studio1 team across social content, memes, product updates, and campaign assets. You will build a visible portfolio around AI, devtools, SaaS, launches, and social growth.

## Internship details

This is a 3-month remote internship for India-based candidates, with extension possible based on performance and work quality. Best fit: someone who enjoys making visuals or videos and wants to learn tech marketing by shipping.

## How to apply

Apply with the form on this page. Include your resume, past work links if you have them, and one clean short doc with a 1-week visual/video content plan for Studio1, Raah, or AssetSnip. You do not need to cover every format; focus only on the formats you are strongest at, such as memes, reels, Shorts, carousels, visual explainers, Canva graphics, AI-video experiments, or product clips.

We usually respond within 1 week if there is a fit.

**We ask for this because even a few minutes spent understanding Studio1 before applying shows you are serious, willing to make an effort, and ready to learn with us.**
