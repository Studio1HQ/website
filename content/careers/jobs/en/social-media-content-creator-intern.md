## What you will do

You will work with the Studio1 team to turn research, product updates, blogs, and technical ideas into written social content that developers and software teams actually want to read.

- Create posts, hooks, memes, X threads, LinkedIn page posts, captions, short scripts, tech explainers, and community prompts for developer audiences.
- Research new AI products, devtool launches, technical concepts, community discussions, social trends, and developer memes to find content angles.
- Manage the weekly social calendar, schedule posts, and help grow Studio1 community channels.
- Support Studio1 marketing content, blog ideas, or lightweight drafts when the topic fits your writing strength.

## What we are looking for

You do not need to know everything. You should be curious, online, and able to show that you have tried writing or managing content before.

- You follow tech, AI, startups, creators, or developer culture closely.
- A tech or software background helps a lot, but curiosity and willingness to learn matter too.
- You have proof of written content from personal pages, campus pages, communities, newsletters, scripts, or past projects.
- You have some interest or experience in SEO writing, content marketing, or writing posts that make people click and read.
- You can spot trends early, write simple hooks, and turn rough ideas into posts, scripts, or short content quickly.

## Why join

You will work closely with the Studio1 team across content, campaigns, and product updates. If you are good at writing or SEO, you may also help with Studio1 blog ideas and drafts.
