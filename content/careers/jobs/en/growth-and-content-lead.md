Studio1 does DevRel and developer marketing for devtool, AI, and open-source teams. We have worked with 35+ teams and driven 3M+ content views. Content without distribution is just a draft sitting in a folder, so we are hiring someone to own how our work, and our clients' work, gets found and shared.

This is a growth role for technical products. Writing more is secondary. Your job is to get the right people to see our content and turn that attention into leads. Technical content and developer communities are changing fast, and we want you to bring a fresh perspective and build the system we run on.

## Why this role exists

Two posts from X that sum up how we think about it. Community, founder-led content, and social media are the channels that work for early-stage tech companies, and the answer to AI slop is not posting less. It's posting better, more often.

## What you will do

- Find content angles and hooks that make technical work spread: launches, open-source projects, tutorials, benchmarks, and opinions.
- Keep every profile active and consistent: Studio1's channels (mainly X and LinkedIn, plus our blog, newsletter, and short video) and the founders' personal profiles.
- Create and ship content yourself, and coordinate with the right person on the team when a piece needs design, video, or engineering input.
- Make founder-led content work: pull the founders' experiences and opinions out of them and turn them into posts, without the founders writing everything.
- Distribute content where developers already are: Reddit, Hacker News, Dev.to, and Discord and Slack communities, within each community's rules.
- Turn one strong piece into many: a thread, a post, a clip, a community post, and a newsletter section.
- Bring the same playbook to client launches and campaigns.
- Use AI tools such as Claude Code, Grok, ChatGPT, and automation to research, draft, and repurpose faster. Cut anything that reads generic, so we sound like real people with real opinions.
- Build our content and distribution system from scratch for how technical content and communities work now: ideas, briefs, drafts, edits, publishing, and repurposing on a weekly rhythm, so output doesn't depend on a good week.
- Lead a lean team. Coordinate with our engineers, writers, designers, and video team, and hire and guide interns as needed to scale our content.
- Notice platform and format shifts early, such as what X rewards now or carousels versus text on LinkedIn, and adjust every week.
- Review what worked every week and do more of it. We measure inbound DMs, calls booked, client leads, and replies and follows from the right people, not views alone.

## What we are looking for

- 1+ year in growth, content, or marketing for a technical product: a devtool, AI, SaaS, API, or open-source project. Experience only with general consumer brands is not a fit.
- Proof in public. Your own X or LinkedIn, or a channel you grew, shows posts or campaigns that spread, and you can explain why they worked.
- You think distribution-first and have taste. You can rewrite a flat hook and say why in one sentence.
- You are comfortable with technical topics. You can read docs and READMEs and talk to engineers.
- You use AI tools every day and check what they produce.
- You know how developer communities react to brands, and what not to post: no spam, no vote manipulation.
- Short-form video, newsletter, or SEO experience is a plus, and so is having guided interns or freelancers.

## Role details

This is a full-time remote role for India-based candidates. You will work closely with the founders and with our engineers, writers, and designers.
