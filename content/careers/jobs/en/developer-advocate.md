Studio1 is looking for a Developer Advocate who can help turn developer products into clear education, useful content, community touchpoints, light demos, and well-coordinated DevRel execution. This role sits beside the DevRel Engineer role, but the focus is different: less deep code ownership, more developer understanding, content planning, feedback loops, internal coordination, and occasional hands-on demo support.

This is not a travel-heavy conference role. Most work is remote and team-based: helping Studio1 plan, execute, review, and distribute DevRel work for Studio1, Raah, AssetSnip, and client projects. Events, talks, or live community sessions may happen sometimes, but they are not the core of the job.

## What you will do

You will work with Studio1's DevRel, content, design, video, and social teams to keep developer-facing work useful, technically accurate, and moving.

- Turn product context, technical research, and client goals into content ideas, outlines, scripts, posts, launch plans, and community angles.
- Help coordinate internal DevRel tasks across blogs, tutorials, videos, docs, demos, social posts, and launch assets.
- Build, adapt, or test small demos, example workflows, notebooks, or API snippets from time to time when it helps the team explain a product better.
- Review technical content for clarity, flow, developer usefulness, positioning, and whether it answers the right developer questions.
- Work with DevRel Engineers and technical writers so demos, tutorials, videos, and campaign assets connect cleanly.
- Join developer/community conversations online when useful, capture feedback, and turn recurring questions into better content or docs ideas.
- Support launches with practical developer messaging, product explanations, posts, short scripts, and follow-up content.
- Help maintain simple internal systems for tracking DevRel tasks, content status, feedback, distribution, and learnings.
- Support client-facing DevRel work by understanding the product, summarizing context, preparing briefs, and helping the team execute consistently.

## What we are looking for

You should understand developers, communicate clearly, and enjoy making technical products easier to understand.

- 1+ year of experience in developer advocacy, DevRel, technical content, community, product marketing for devtools, open source, or a similar role is preferred.
- You can understand AI, devtools, SaaS, APIs, open-source projects, and developer workflows well enough to plan useful content, ask good technical questions, and occasionally work with code examples or demos.
- You have enough coding comfort to run projects, test examples, edit snippets, follow docs, and ship small demos when needed, even if you are not the main engineer on larger builds.
- You write clearly and can turn rough notes, product context, or research into structured outlines, posts, scripts, and briefs.
- You are comfortable coordinating across writers, engineers, designers, video editors, founders, and clients.
- You can review content from a developer's point of view and spot where something is unclear, too promotional, too shallow, or missing practical context.
- You can participate in online developer communities with good judgment and without forcing salesy messaging.
- You are organized enough to track many small moving pieces across content, launches, community, and client work.
- You can share proof of work such as developer content, community work, launch campaigns, technical posts, videos, docs, events, open-source/community contributions, or strong writing samples.

## Why join

You will work on real developer products across AI, agents, devtools, open source, and SaaS. You will help shape how Studio1 divides DevRel work internally: engineers own the deeper technical builds, while advocates help turn that work into clear education, community learning, launch support, developer adoption, and occasional lightweight demos.

## Role details

This is a full-time remote role for India-based candidates. Best fit: developer advocates, DevRel generalists, technical community builders, technical content strategists, devtool product marketers, or strong communicators who understand developer products and can work with code when needed.
