Studio1 does DevRel and developer content for AI, devtool, and open-source companies. We are hiring four contract technical writers who are engineers first: you build the AI agent or GenAI app, get it working, and then write the article that shows other developers how to do it.

This is not a role for content writers who learn the topic from the docs and write around it. Every article starts with working code you wrote yourself.

## What you will build and write

- Build working demos with AI agents and GenAI: tool calling, multi-step agents, multi-agent setups, RAG, memory, MCP servers, structured outputs, evals, and model APIs.
- Use agent frameworks and SDKs, and learn new ones fast enough to build accurate examples.
- Write tutorials, integration guides, and deep dives from what you built, with the code in a public repo developers can run.
- Explain the choices: why this approach, what broke, what the tradeoffs are, and what you'd do in production.
- Work with our editors and technical reviewers, and take feedback well.

## What we are looking for

- Past experience writing for technical companies is a must. Send 2-4 articles you wrote that a devtool, AI, or software company published on its blog or docs. Articles only on your personal blog, Medium, or Dev.to don't count for this role.
- You are an engineer. You write Python or TypeScript well and have built AI or LLM projects beyond a basic chatbot or API wrapper.
- You write your own articles. AI can help you research or debug, but the writing has to be yours. We can tell, and we read every sample.
- You explain clearly: a developer can follow your article from start to finish and end with something that works.
- High agency: you don't wait to be told what to do. You spot what needs doing, figure it out, ship it, and own the result.
- A plus: open-source contributions, a technical audience of your own, or experience writing docs.

## Contract details

This is a remote contract role, open to writers anywhere in the world, paid per article. We are hiring four writers. Applications will open soon.

## How to apply

Applications for this role will open soon. When they open, have these ready:

1. 2-4 articles you wrote that were published on a company's blog or docs, not your personal blog.
2. A repo you built for one of them, or another AI project you built.
3. Your expected pay per article.

We review your published articles, your code, and your rate, then let you know whether we want to go ahead. Once applications open, we usually respond within 1 week if there is a fit.

**We ask for company-published articles because they show your work held up to a real company's technical review.**
