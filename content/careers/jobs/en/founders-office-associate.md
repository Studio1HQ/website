Studio1 is looking for a high-agency Founder's Office Associate who can work closely with the founders across operations, team coordination, growth, marketing, sales, and internal execution. This role is for someone who notices gaps, creates structure, follows through, and helps priorities move from discussion to execution.

## What you will do

You will work directly with the founders to improve how Studio1 operates, keep teams aligned, and support company growth.

- Build and own internal systems for tracking projects, tasks, deadlines, team progress, and company priorities.
- Coordinate across content, social, DevRel, design, engineering, and other teams by assigning tasks, following up, reviewing progress, and keeping execution moving.
- Own client outreach support, sales follow-ups, lead lists, CRM or spreadsheet tracking, and pipeline updates for Studio1 services and products.
- Support growth, marketing, client research, partnerships, and other initiatives that help Studio1, Raah, and AssetSnip grow.
- Draft outreach emails, follow-up notes, marketing blog drafts, social media copy, and product update posts when a growth or marketing task needs writing support.
- Coordinate marketing blog ideas, topic lists, publishing tasks, campaign tasks, product updates, and content packaging with the Studio1 team.
- Improve internal workflows, documentation, reporting, and processes while identifying blockers early and making sure important work does not get stuck.

## What we are looking for

You should be organized, proactive, comfortable taking ownership, and able to work across different parts of a company without waiting for every next step.

- 1+ year of experience in a startup, operations, founder's office, growth, project management, or a similar role is preferred.
- Strong candidates with less formal experience but demonstrated ownership through startups, projects, communities, hackathons, or other work are also encouraged to apply.
- You can take a loosely defined problem, figure out what needs to happen, ask the right questions, and drive it to completion.
- You are comfortable coordinating people, assigning and following up on tasks, and keeping multiple projects organized.
- You can build and manage workflows using Notion, Linear, spreadsheets, CRMs, or similar tools.
- You can research companies, potential clients, competitors, markets, and new growth opportunities.
- You can support marketing execution, outreach tracking, blog/topic coordination, campaign planning, and sales follow-ups with strong attention to detail.
- You can write clear outreach emails, short marketing copy, social post drafts, and simple blog drafts without needing every line handed to you.
- You communicate clearly, share updates before being asked, and can keep work moving in a remote team.
- A tech or software background helps, especially if you understand AI, SaaS, devtools, or developer products.

## Why join

You will work closely with the founders and see how a growing tech company actually operates. You will get hands-on ownership across operations, internal systems, team execution, sales, marketing, outreach, partnerships, and growth instead of being limited to one function.

## Role details

This is a full-time remote role for India-based candidates. Best fit: early-career operators who have worked in startups, managed projects or teams, built internal systems, or taken ownership of execution across multiple people.

## How to apply

Apply with the form on this page. Include your resume, past work links if you have them, and one clean short doc showing how you would approach your first week at Studio1. Tell us what you would organize, improve, track, or start doing across operations, outreach, sales, growth, marketing, or internal execution after understanding our company and work.

We usually respond within 1 week if there is a fit.

**We ask for this because this is a high-agency role. We want people who can understand how things work, identify what can be improved, and take ownership without waiting for every next step.**
