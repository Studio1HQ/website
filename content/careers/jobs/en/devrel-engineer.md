Studio1 is hiring two DevRel Engineers from a GenAI and agentic AI background. You will build agents and AI apps around developer products, write and fix their docs, and test new features before developers hit them.

This is an engineering role first. We want someone who ships working agent projects, understands how they break, and can explain them clearly in docs and tutorials.

## What you will do

You will work on developer products for Studio1 and our clients across AI, devtools, and open source.

- Build AI agents and agentic apps: tool calling, multi-step workflows, memory, RAG, MCP servers, and multi-agent setups.
- Build integrations, sample apps, and reference repos around developer products.
- Write and improve documentation: quickstarts, API and SDK guides, READMEs, and integration docs.
- Test new features and releases as a developer would: follow the docs, find what breaks or confuses, and report it clearly with fixes.
- Write tutorials and guides from what you built, and record a short walkthrough when a topic needs video.
- Learn new agent frameworks, SDKs, model APIs, and coding agents fast enough to build accurate examples.
- Use Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, and other coding agents to prototype fast, and check that what ships actually works.
- Help with partnerships and community initiatives: joint demos and integrations with other developer companies, hackathons, and developer community activities.
- Review technical content from the team for accuracy.

## What we are looking for

- A GenAI and agentic AI background is a must. You have built and shipped AI agent or LLM projects beyond a basic chatbot or API wrapper. Show us the repos.
- High agency: you don't wait to be told what to do. You spot what needs doing, figure it out, ship it, and own the result.
- You write solid Python or TypeScript and are comfortable with LLM APIs, tool calling, RAG, structured outputs, and basic evals.
- You write clear docs. A developer can follow your quickstart or README without asking you a question.
- You notice the details when testing a product: the missing step, the wrong default, the error message that doesn't help.
- You use coding agents every day and know where they help and where they fail.
- 1+ year in AI engineering, DevRel engineering, open source, or a similar technical role is preferred. Strong public projects count as much as job titles.
- A plus: open-source contributions, a technical blog, or experience working with partners or developer communities.

## Why join

You will build real agentic projects for AI, devtool, and open-source products and own them end to end: build, document, test, and ship to developers.

## Role details

This is a full-time remote role for India-based candidates. You will work closely with the founders and the entire team.

## How to apply

Apply with the form on this page. Have these ready:

1. Your resume and GitHub profile.
2. Links to two or three GenAI or agentic AI projects you built: what each does, how it's built, and a demo if there is one.
3. Docs, a README, or a tutorial you wrote.
4. One short doc: pick an AI developer tool, try its quickstart and one newer feature, and tell us what broke or confused you and how you'd fix the docs.

We usually respond within 1 week if there is a fit.

**We ask for proof because this role is about building: working agent projects, clear docs, and careful testing.**
