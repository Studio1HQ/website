Studio1 is hiring a DevRel Engineer who builds with AI every day. You will build AI agents, agentic apps, and integrations around developer products, then turn that work into tutorials, videos, and launches that developers actually use.

This is an engineering role first. We are not looking for an advocate who mainly talks about products. We want someone who ships working agent projects, understands how they break, and can explain them clearly. Thinking about distribution while you build is part of the job: what makes a demo worth sharing, and where developers will see it.

The other half of the role is people. You will represent Studio1 and our clients at events, give talks, and build partnerships and collaborations with other developer companies.

This role is the whole of DevRel, not one slice of it. If you only want to write blogs, or only want to go to events, it isn't the right fit. We want someone who builds, writes, records, shows up in developer communities, works with partners, and brings what developers say back into the product and the content.

## What you will do

You will work with the Studio1 team on developer products for Studio1, Raah, AssetSnip, and our clients across AI, devtools, and open source.

- Build AI agents and agentic apps: tool calling, multi-step workflows, memory, RAG, and multi-agent setups.
- Build MCP servers, integrations, sample apps, and reference repos around developer products.
- Learn new agent frameworks, SDKs, model APIs, and coding agents fast enough to build accurate examples, such as the OpenAI Agents SDK, Claude Agent SDK, LangGraph, CrewAI, Mastra, and MCP.
- Use Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, and other coding agents to prototype fast, and check that what ships actually works.
- Write tutorials, guides, and docs from what you built, and record demo walkthroughs when a topic needs video.
- Package repos and demos for GitHub, X, Reddit, Hacker News, and YouTube, and work with our Growth Lead on hooks and timing.
- Join developer conversations, capture feedback, and turn recurring questions into better demos, content, or docs.
- Represent Studio1 and our clients at meetups, conferences, and hackathons, online and in person.
- Find the right conferences, submit CFPs, and give talks and workshops on what you built.
- Build partnerships with devtool and AI companies: reach out, set up collaborations such as joint demos, integrations, co-hosted events, and content swaps, and follow through.
- Review technical content from the team for accuracy.

## What we are looking for

- High agency: you don't wait to be told what to do. You spot what needs doing, figure it out, ship it, and own the result.
- You have built and shipped AI agent or LLM projects beyond a basic chatbot or API wrapper. Show us the repos.
- You write solid Python or TypeScript and are comfortable with LLM APIs, tool calling, RAG, structured outputs, and basic evals.
- You use coding agents every day and know where they help and where they fail.
- You can explain what you built in a clear tutorial, README, or short video.
- You like meeting people and can start and follow through on conversations with other companies.
- You have instincts for what developers will click, star, and share.
- 1+ year in AI engineering, DevRel engineering, open source, or a similar technical role is preferred. Strong public projects count as much as job titles.
- A strong plus: you are comfortable speaking in public and representing a company to developers and partners, or you have given talks, had CFPs accepted, contributed to open source, or run a technical blog or developer community.

## Why join

You will build real agentic projects for AI, devtool, and open-source products and own them end to end: build, write, record, and ship to developers. You will also build your own name in the developer community through talks, events, and partnerships.

## Role details

This is a full-time remote role for India-based candidates. You will work closely with the founders, our Growth Lead and Content Lead, and our writers and designers.
