Studio1 will soon open a DevRel Engineer role for builders with GenAI and AI-agent experience who can build, explain, and ship developer-facing work across AI, devtools, and open-source products. This is an engineering-first role that also covers advocacy and content: you build the demo, write the tutorial, and help make sure developers actually see it.

The core work is technical. You build projects, understand developer products, and write and record content from real implementation work. What sets this role apart is thinking about distribution while you build: what makes a demo worth sharing, which angle will land with developers, and where it should go.

## What you will do

You will work with the Studio1 team on DevRel work for Studio1, Raah, AssetSnip, and client projects.

- Build AI-agent demos, GenAI workflows, sample apps, integrations, and prototype repos around developer products.
- Learn new frameworks, SDKs, APIs, model providers, and agent tools quickly enough to create accurate examples.
- Use Claude Code and other coding agents to prototype fast, and check that what ships actually works.
- Write technical blogs, tutorials, docs, guides, and launch content from real implementation work.
- Record demo walkthroughs, product explainers, and short technical videos when a topic needs video.
- Turn product context and technical research into angles, outlines, scripts, posts, and launch plans.
- Package demos and repos for X, Reddit, Hacker News, YouTube, and developer communities, and work with our Growth Lead on hooks and timing.
- Join developer conversations online, capture feedback, and turn recurring questions into better content or docs.
- Review technical content from the team for accuracy, clarity, and whether it answers the questions developers are asking.

## What we are looking for

You should be comfortable writing code, explaining technical ideas, and learning unfamiliar tools without waiting for step-by-step instructions.

- 1+ year of experience in DevRel engineering, developer advocacy, GenAI engineering, AI-agent projects, technical content, open source, or a similar technical role is preferred.
- You can build practical demos around AI agents, LLMs, RAG, tool calling, model APIs, SDKs, auth, devtools, or infrastructure, beyond generic web apps or wrapper demos.
- You can write clear technical blogs, tutorials, docs, or guides from real implementation work.
- You have instincts for what developers will click, star, and share, and you can turn a technical idea into a demo, post, or video that spreads.
- You are comfortable planning, recording, and explaining technical walkthroughs.
- You can talk with developers, understand feedback, and represent technical work clearly.
- Previous work with startups, clients, open-source projects, or developer communities is a strong plus.
- You can share proof of work such as GitHub repos, live demos, technical blogs, videos, open-source contributions, or posts that reached developers.

## Why join

You will work on real developer products and launches across AI, agents, devtools, and open source. You will get ownership to build, write, record, and distribute work that developers actually use.

## Role details

This is a full-time remote role for India-based candidates. Applications will open soon. Best fit: DevRel engineers, developer advocates who code, GenAI builders, AI-agent developers, and technical creators.
