Studio1 publishes a lot of developer content: tutorials, integration guides, and deep dives for AI, devtool, and open-source companies, written by engineers around the world. We are hiring five contract Technical Content Reviewers to check every piece before it goes out.

Your job is to make sure each article is correct, easy to find, and sounds like us. You catch the broken code sample, the claim that isn't true, the heading that won't rank, and the sentence that reads like AI, and you send it back with clear notes or fix it yourself.

## What you will do

- Review articles for technical accuracy: run or read the code, check commands and versions, and flag claims that don't hold up.
- Check every piece for SEO, AEO, and GEO: the right search intent, clear headings, direct answers near the top, FAQs where they help, and structure that search engines and AI answers can pick up.
- Fix grammar, clarity, and structure, and cut filler and generic AI-style writing.
- Make sure each piece follows our brand and style guidelines, and help keep those guidelines up to date.
- Give writers specific, kind feedback they can act on, and track the mistakes that keep coming back.
- Work with our writers so pieces ship on time.

## What we are looking for

- High agency: you don't wait to be told what to do. You spot what needs doing, figure it out, ship it, and own the result.
- You have edited or reviewed technical content before: blogs, tutorials, or docs for a software company.
- You have a technical background. You can read code in JavaScript, TypeScript, or Python, run a tutorial yourself, and tell when something is wrong.
- You know how SEO works for developer content, and you follow how AI answers such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google's AI results pick their sources.
- Your English is excellent, and you can explain an edit in one sentence.
- You notice small things and still keep the writer's voice.
- A plus: you have written technical articles yourself, or you have worked with a style guide.

## Contract details

This is a remote contract role for India-based candidates, and we are hiring five reviewers. We can work per hour or per article, depending on what suits you and the volume. Applications will open soon.

## How to apply

Applications for this role will open soon. When they open, have these ready:

1. Technical articles you have edited or reviewed, and what you changed.
2. Your review of one post from the Studio1 blog, as comments in a shared doc.
3. Your expected pay per article reviewed, in INR. If you'd rather work per hour, say so in your application.

Once applications open, we usually respond within 1 week if there is a fit.

**We ask for a sample review because it shows exactly how you edit, and that's the work we want to see.**
