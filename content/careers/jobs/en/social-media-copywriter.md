Studio1 does DevRel and developer marketing for devtool, AI, and open-source teams. A lot of that work happens on LinkedIn and X: company pages, founder profiles, launches, and the posts that make developers and buyers remember a name.

We are looking for a copywriter who has already done this for someone else. You have run a company's LinkedIn page or X account, or ghostwritten for a founder, and you can show the posts and what they did. You will write for Studio1, our founders, and our clients' founders and company accounts.

This role is open as an internship or a contract. Pick the one that fits you when you apply.

## What you will do

- Write LinkedIn and X posts for Studio1's company accounts and the founders' personal profiles, every week.
- Ghostwrite for our clients' founders and company pages, in each person's own voice.
- Turn calls, notes, launches, blogs, and case studies into posts: short posts, threads, carousels, and X articles.
- Write hooks that stop the scroll and posts that get replies from the right people, not just likes.
- Keep a posting calendar for each account, schedule posts, and keep every profile active and consistent.
- Watch what's working on LinkedIn and X each week, and adjust formats, topics, and timing.

## What we are looking for

- High agency: you don't wait to be told what to do. You spot what needs doing, figure it out, ship it, and own the result.
- Proof first. You have written for at least one company page or founder profile on LinkedIn or X, and you can share the links and the results. Only posting on your own profile isn't enough for this role.
- You can write in someone else's voice so it sounds like them, not like a brand.
- You know how LinkedIn and X differ: what each one rewards, the formats that work, and what makes a post feel like an ad.
- You follow tech, AI, and startups, and can write about developer products without getting the details wrong.
- You use AI tools to research and draft faster, and you rewrite anything that reads generic.
- A plus: writing for devtool, AI, or SaaS founders, carousels or X articles, or an audience you grew yourself.

## Why join

You will write for founders and companies across AI, devtools, and open source, and see what works across many accounts at once. You will work closely with the founders and the entire team, and the people who do well here get more accounts and more ownership.

## Role details

This is a remote role. The internship is for India-based candidates. The contract is open to anyone who can overlap with Indian working hours. We agree on hours and pay with you based on the track and your experience.

## How to apply

Apply with the form on this page. Have these ready:

1. Whether you want the internship, the contract, or either.
2. The company pages or founder profiles you have written for, with links and results.
3. Three sample posts: a LinkedIn post and an X post for a devtool founder, and one post for Studio1's company page.

We usually respond within 1 week if there is a fit.

**We ask for accounts you have written for because this role is about writing in someone else's voice, and that's the work we want to see.**
