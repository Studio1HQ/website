Studio1 is hiring its first Sales Development Representative. You will build our outbound pipeline from the ground up: who we reach out to, when, and what we say. You will work directly with the founders, who take the calls you book.

## Why this role exists

Devtool and AI startups need developer marketing at specific moments: right after a funding round, before a launch, when they post their first DevRel or growth role, or when a competitor's tutorials start showing up everywhere. Reach a team at that moment with something relevant and a first call is easy. Reach them a month later and it isn't.

This role is about spotting those moments and starting the conversation. Before you write to anyone, read a few of our [case studies](/case-studies): that is the work you'll be pointing people to.

## What you will do

- Build lists of companies we can help, from signals that mean "now": new funding, product launches, hiring for DevRel or developer marketing, new SDKs and APIs, accelerator batches such as YC, and ecosystems we already know well.
- Find the right person at each company, usually a founder, the head of marketing or growth, or the DevRel lead.
- Write outreach that shows you have looked at their product: one specific observation, one relevant example of our work, and an easy next step.
- Run email and LinkedIn sequences at a steady daily pace, and keep our sending in good shape: clean lists, sensible volumes, warmed-up mailboxes, and opt-outs honoured.
- Show up where our buyers already are: devtool and AI communities, founder groups, and follow-ups after events.
- Book discovery calls for the founders and hand over the context: what the company does, why now, and what we already know.
- Test subject lines, angles and offers by segment (AI infrastructure, AI agents, platform engineering, data tools, open source) and keep what works.
- Keep every account and conversation up to date in our CRM, and share a short report each week.

## What we are looking for

- High agency: you don't wait to be told what to do. You spot what needs doing, figure it out, ship it, and own the result.
- 1 to 2 years in an SDR, BDR or outbound sales role, ideally selling to software or technical teams.
- You have run cold outreach at volume and can show it worked: reply rates, meetings booked, a sequence you're proud of.
- Hands-on with prospecting tools such as Apollo and LinkedIn Sales Navigator, a CRM, and an email sequencing tool.
- Short, clear writing in English. Your emails don't read like templates, even when they start from one.
- Curious about developer tools and AI. You can read a landing page, the docs or a GitHub README and explain what the product does and who it's for.
- You use AI tools every day to research and draft faster, and you check what they produce.
- A plus: you have sold services or software to developer-focused companies, or you already know the devtool and AI startup scene.

## How we'll measure it

- Every week: accounts researched, people reached on email and LinkedIn, reply rate, and conversations started.
- Every month: qualified discovery calls booked, and how many of them turn into proposals.

We care about the quality of a meeting more than raw volume. No bought lists sent blindly and no thousand-email blasts with the same copy.

## Role details

This is a full-time remote role for India-based candidates. Many of the teams you'll reach are in the US and Europe, so you'll plan sends and follow-ups around their time zones. You'll work with the founders, our Sales Engineer (we're hiring for that role too), and the team that delivers the work you sell.

## How to apply

Apply with the form on this page. Have these ready:

1. A cold email you wrote that got a reply (remove anything private), and what it led to.
2. One devtool or AI startup that raised money in the last three months: why they might need developer marketing now, who you would contact, and the first email you would send them.
3. Your numbers from a past outbound role, if you can share them: volume, reply rate and meetings booked.

We usually respond within 1 week if there is a fit.

**We care more about judgment and proof of work than about a perfect CV.**
