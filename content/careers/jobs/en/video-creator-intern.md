Studio1 does DevRel and developer marketing for devtool, AI, and open-source teams. We work with new tech every week: new LLMs, new frameworks, new developer tools. We publish videos every day on YouTube, Instagram, X, and LinkedIn, and we are hiring a creative intern who knows the tech to make it happen.

You know what you're talking about, and you find a fresh, interesting way to teach it. Tutorials, explainers, quick takes on a new release, a talk-style video on a trend: if it teaches the audience something new and people want to watch it, it fits.

This is not for someone who wants to start making videos. It's for someone who already makes tech videos and wants to do it as a role. The internship is the start of a full-time position: grow our channels and it becomes a full-time hire.

You don't need advanced editing skills. A good idea, clear explanation, clean cuts, and good pacing matter more than effects.

## What you will do

- Record and publish daily videos for Studio1's channels: tutorials, explainers, tool walkthroughs, and quick takes on new LLMs, frameworks, and developer tools.
- Try new tech yourself before you explain it, so the video shows something real that works.
- Come up with creative formats and angles that make a technical topic exciting to watch.
- Cut Shorts and reels from longer videos and recordings, and post them on YouTube, Instagram, X, and LinkedIn.
- Research topics and trends: new model releases, frameworks, and launches, what developers are talking about this week, and questions worth answering.
- Write simple scripts and outlines, and keep them technically accurate. Ask the team when you're unsure.
- Do basic edits: cuts, captions, zooms, music, and thumbnails.
- Track what works, such as views, watch time, and comments, and make more of it.

## What we are looking for

- High agency: you don't wait to be told what to do. You spot what needs doing, figure it out, ship it, and own the result.
- Creative: you have your own ideas for videos and a sense of what makes people keep watching.
- A tech background and coding knowledge are a must. You can build or run what you're showing, so you explain it like a developer, not a marketer.
- You follow tech closely: new LLMs, AI tools, frameworks, and developer trends.
- Comfortable trying new things, from new tools to new video formats, and shipping them.
- Fluent spoken English, and comfortable recording yourself on camera and on screen.
- Basic editing skills in any tool: CapCut, DaVinci Resolve, Premiere Pro, Descript, or similar. We are not looking for advanced motion design or heavy effects.
- Consistency: you can ship something every day.
- You already make videos. Show us tutorials, explainers, Shorts, reels, or a channel you run. Videos made for companies are a plus.
- College students are welcome and preferred, as long as you are already making videos.

## Why join

You will be among the first to try new models, frameworks, and developer tools, build a public portfolio of videos on them, and grow with the channel.

## Internship details

This is a remote internship for India-based candidates, and the role is scoped for a full-time hire. You will work closely with the founders. If you grow our channels, you become our full-time video creator.

## How to apply

Apply with the form on this page. Have these ready:

1. Links to videos you have created: tutorials, explainers, Shorts, reels, or your channel. Work for companies is a plus, and numbers help.
2. A 60-second video of you explaining a new LLM, framework, or developer tool you've tried.
3. Five video ideas for Studio1's channels on current tech trends.

We usually respond within 1 week if there is a fit.

**We care more about your videos, ideas, and consistency than about a perfect CV.**
